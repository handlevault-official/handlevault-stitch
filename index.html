How it works

Three steps, no account.

Add your input

Upload an image or fill in a short form.

Adjust the settings

Pick size, colors, template or currency to suit you.

Download the PDF

Print it, share it or keep it. We don’t keep a copy.

Why “sovereign”?

These tools do their work on their own server, so your data isn’t passed around.

No third-party APIs

Your images and details are never sent to outside services to be processed.

Processed in memory

Files are turned into a PDF and discarded. There is no database of your uploads.

Your data stays with you

Saved details, like your business name or resume, live in your own browser only.

Questions

Is it really free?

You can try every tool for free. Premium options, such as removing the footer or watermark and saving history, may be added later.

Do you keep my images or resume?

No. Uploads are processed in memory to make your PDF and are not saved on the server. Anything you choose to save, like your resume draft, stays in your own browser.

Are the thread and bead colors exact?

They are close matches, not an exact chart for every brand. Compare with your own threads or beads before you start a big project.

Will employers’ systems read my resume?

The PDF uses real, selectable text and a simple layout, which is what applicant tracking systems generally need.
